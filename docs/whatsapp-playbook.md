# WhatsApp playbook — reply templates

Goal: answer fast, sound human, move to a concrete quote. English first.
No walls of emoji. Never quote a price that isn't in `data/services.json`.

## First reply (any inquiry)

> Hey! Thanks for reaching out 🙌 Happy to help. Could you send me your dates,
> how many people, and where you're staying in Nosara? I'll get you options
> right away.

## Transportation inquiry (NFT)

> We do private door-to-door rides — AC, room for luggage and surfboards.
> Local Nosara rides start at $60. Where are you headed and what day?

(Only give exact route prices from `data/services.json`. If the route isn't
listed: "Let me confirm that one for you — give me a few minutes.")

## NJA experience inquiry

> Great choice — that one's a favorite. It's [price from services.json].
> What dates are you thinking? I'll check availability and we can lock it in.

## Turtle arribada — set expectations honestly

> The arribada is a natural event — it follows the tides and the turtles'
> own cycle, so nobody can promise an exact night. What we do is track the
> conditions and take you when the chances are best. It's $75 per person,
> minimum 2 guests.

## When we don't have the info yet

> Good question — let me confirm that with the team so I give you the exact
> answer, not a guess. Back to you shortly.

## Closing a booking

> Perfect, you're set for [date] ✓ I'll send a confirmation here the day
> before. Anything else you need for the trip, just message me. Pura vida!
