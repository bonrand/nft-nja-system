# Brand system — quick reference

Full brief lives in `CLAUDE.md`. This is the cheat sheet for day-to-day production.

## Two brands, one family

| | NFT — Nosara Fun Travel | NJA — Nosara Jungle Adventures |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Trusted local front door | Premium experience line |
| Tone | Warm, direct, fast, human | Calm, luminous, editorial, sensory |
| Sells | Transportation, tours, guest support | ATV/UTV, waterfalls, arribada, coffee |
| CTAs | Message us · Send your dates · Plan your stay | Book on WhatsApp · Check dates · Let's plan it together |

Bridge line: "Nosara Fun Travel connects you with the best local transportation
and private experiences. Nosara Jungle Adventures is our curated adventure side."

## Palette (defined once in `templates/styles.css`)

- Warm cream `#F7F1E6` — canvas
- Deep jungle `#234D38` — primary, CTA pill
- Teal water `#2E8C84` — accents
- Golden sun `#DFA53B` — small highlights
- Clay earth `#BD6B45` — **placeholder/unconfirmed text only on flyers**
- Ink `#1C3A2C` — body text

## Type

Fraunces (display serif) + Hanken Grotesk (body).
In Canva: Playfair Display / DM Serif + Mulish.

## Flyer anatomy (1080×1350)

1. Hero photo, top ~57% — real photo, soft dark gradient at the bottom
2. Brand mark top-left · thin pill badge top-right
3. Serif headline sitting on the photo
4. Cream content area ~43%: one short description → 2–3 chips → price + CTA → footer
5. One hook, one photo, one price, one CTA. That's it.

## Hard rules

- No dark/black backgrounds on public NJA flyers (private quote flyer is the only exception).
- Never invent prices, durations, inclusions, policies. Unconfirmed = visible [brackets].
- Never guarantee the turtle arribada — it's a natural event, frame it respectfully.
- Banned words: cheap, best prices, discount.
- General flyers never show the full route list — that's the rate sheet's job.
